The corrected isolated FE dedendum response is 0.178987 µm RMS, compared with 0.196589 µm analytically. The earlier ninefold contrast mixed addendum and dedendum modifications and is superseded. Restricted fits do not identify dedendum presence or geometry in the tested cross-operator data. Version 13 adds waveform and phase-mask controls, a fixed-body pressure-interface study, physically modified contours with reassembled stiffness, and verification of 42 bibliographic entries. The article makes conditional observability claims, not validated physical profile recovery.

The package contains scripts, numerical records, 21 supplementary tables, verification reports and checksums. Its README explains execution and evidence boundaries. Forty unit tests pass; all 1800 archived statistical fits were audited but not all were refitted.

[The historical manuscript-v10 tag](https://github.com/romusan/gears-inversion-reproducibility/tree/manuscript-v10) remains unchanged. Its historical claims must be read with the corrections in version 13. No DOI is assigned to the new package.

Version 13 adds numerical controls on the same eight physical-removal phases. Alternative algebra, weighted interior mesh extension and halved pressure spacing change the centred contrast by at most 0.019725 nm RMS (0.949% of the same-depth reference). These empirical controls are not rigorous total-error bounds, experimental detectability evidence, or a full-cycle inverse convergence certificate. Missing transition observations may contain additional information.

[Historical version 12](https://github.com/romusan/gears-inversion-reproducibility/tree/manuscript-v12) is preserved. The local submission kit includes the article, supplementary document, editable Highlights and a verified reproducibility ZIP.
